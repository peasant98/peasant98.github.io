abstract: We introduce Recurrent All-Pairs Field Transforms (RAFT), a new deep network architecture for optical flow. RAFT extracts per-pixel features, builds multi-scale 4D correlation volumes for all pairs of pixels, and iteratively updates a flow field through a recurrent unit that performs lookups on the correlation volumes. RAFT achieves state-of-the-art performance. On KITTI, RAFT achieves an F1-all error of 5.10%, a 16% error reduction from the best published result (6.10%). On Sintel (final pass), RAFT obtains an end-point-error of 2.855 pixels, a 30% error reduction from the best published result (4.098 pixels). In addition, RAFT has strong cross-dataset generalization as well as high efficiency in inference time, training speed, and parameter count. Code is available at https://github.com/princeton-vl/RAFT.

## What they do
Most deep optical-flow networks work coarse-to-fine, so coarse errors are hard to undo and small fast-moving objects get missed. RAFT keeps one flow field at 1/8 resolution and refines it many times with the same small recurrent unit, which reads a correlation volume between all pairs of pixels.

## How it works
- **Features.** A residual encoder maps both images to 256-dim features at 1/8 resolution. A context encoder runs on the first image only.
- **All-pairs correlation.** One matrix multiply correlates every feature pair. Pooling the second image's dimensions with kernels 1, 2, 4 and 8 gives a 4-level pyramid. Each step bilinearly samples a radius-4 grid around every pixel's current match on all levels.
- **Update operator.** A convolutional GRU, with weights tied across iterations, turns correlation, flow and context features into a flow update. Flow starts at zero. Training unrolls 12 updates.
- **Upsampling and loss.** Full-resolution flow is a learned convex combination of 3x3 coarse neighbours. The L1 loss covers every iterate, weighted toward later ones.

## Results
- Trained only on synthetic data, it reaches 1.43 EPE on Sintel clean (29% below FlowNet2) and cuts KITTI-15 EPE from 8.36 to 5.04.
- It ranks first on both Sintel test passes (1.61 clean, 2.86 final EPE with warm-start) and on KITTI (5.10% F1-all against 6.10%).
- 5.3M parameters, 0.10 s per 1088x436 pair on a 1080Ti, and 200k training iterations against FlowNet2's 7000k. The 1.0M-parameter RAFT-S still beats prior methods on Sintel final (3.37 EPE).
- Ablations: untied weights are less accurate and need 32.5M parameters instead of 4.8M. Warping instead of correlation raises KITTI EPE from 5.54 to 11.83.

## Limitations
The paper does not discuss limitations. All-pairs correlation grows with the square of the pixel count. An on-demand alternative is described but not needed: at 1080p, correlation takes 17% of inference time (550 ms for 12 updates). Evaluation covers only Sintel and KITTI, plus qualitative DAVIS video.
