tldr: ReGeoAnch trains an imitation policy on RGB-D demonstrations from one camera and runs it from an RGB camera placed elsewhere. It predicts depth and pools it onto grids fixed to the robot base and the gripper, then uses appearance to choose which grid cells feed the policy. abstract: 'Moving the camera changes where objects appear in an image, challenging RGB-based imitation learning. A common strategy pairs synthesized views with the same demonstrated action, but ignores supporting physical correspondence across views. By predicting depth with camera calibrations, 3D policies may handle it yet suffer the issue of varied observed point sets. To address these limitations, we propose ReGeoAnch, a geometry-anchored imitation learning framework for viewpoint generalization. Specifically, we build global anchors in the robot base frame and local anchors in the gripper frame, providing shared physical references across viewpoints. Furthermore, Decoupled Appearance-Guided Geometric Readout uses Point-to-Anchor Aggregation (PAA) to obtain comparable and stable geometry around anchors beyond pointwise correspondence. A controllable and decoupled information flow, Anchor-to-Policy Readout (APR), leverages complementary visual and geometric information and supports viewpoint generalization: view-dependent appearances are queried by view-invariant robot state to select task-relevant anchors, with geometric values alone to form the final readout. We further introduce Cross-View Readout Consistency to align the directions of policy readouts from paired source and synthesized target views. Experiments on 12 manipulation tasks across MetaWorld and ManiSkill3 demonstrate improved viewpoint generalization over baseline policies.'

## What they do
Demonstrations come from one calibrated RGB-D camera; the policy is deployed with an RGB camera elsewhere whose calibration is known but which has no demonstrations. Synthesized views alone do not say which physical regions should drive the action, and point-cloud policies see different surfaces from each view. ReGeoAnch reads the scene through anchors fixed in robot coordinates, which mark the same physical place in every view.

## How it works
- **Depth from calibrated RGB.** A light decoder on the policy's ResNet-18 features, conditioned on camera rays, predicts depth; points are placed in the robot base frame.
- **Anchors and pooling.** A global grid in the base frame (972 anchors) and a finer grid moving with the gripper (4,096). Each anchor softly pools nearby points into a geometric descriptor.
- **Decoupled readout.** Attention whose query is the robot state, whose keys combine appearance and geometry, and whose values are geometry only. The resulting readout conditions a Diffusion Policy head.
- **Cross-view consistency.** GenWarp synthesizes target-view images from the source RGB-D. A cosine loss aligns each target-view readout with the source-view readout.

## Results
- Eight MetaWorld tasks (Sawyer) and four ManiSkill3 tasks (Panda), 400 demonstrations each, 100 rollouts per camera pose. Success averages 86.6% and 95.6% on the two benchmarks, 91.1% overall, versus 79.7% for 3D Diffuser Actor and 74.5% for Diffusion Policy trained on the same synthesized views. It is best or tied for best on 9 of 12 tasks.
- On ManiSkill3 viewpoints outside the training range, it reaches 87.0% at -30° azimuth and 56.8% at +30° elevation, versus 43.8% and 27.8% for RAVEN.
- Replacing the learned anchor selection with fixed weights drops success to 59.4%. Swapping the readout for a per-anchor MLP gives 56.5% versus 94.5%. Calibration errors up to 2 mm, 0.2° and 0.5% focal length keep success at or above 93.5%.

## Limitations
All experiments run in simulation at 128×128 resolution, and the target camera's calibration must be known during training. One policy is trained per task and per type of camera change. Gains beyond the training range are smaller for elevation than for azimuth. Under changes in camera distance, RAVEN scores slightly higher (99.8% vs 98.0%). The method adds 14.7 ms per action chunk over Diffusion Policy.
