## What they do
robosuite is a simulation framework and benchmark for robot learning built on MuJoCo. It grew out of the SURREAL distributed-RL project and is now part of the ARISE Initiative. This version of the paper describes the v1.5 release: 10 robot models (including the GR1 humanoid, Spot and TIAGo), 9 grippers (including the Inspire dexterous hand), 4 bases, nine standardized manipulation tasks, and tools for collecting human demonstrations.

## How it works
- **Modeling APIs.** A Task merges one or more robots (each with gripper and base models), an arena and any objects into a single MJCF model for MuJoCo. Objects are loaded from XML or composed procedurally from primitive geoms, and a placement initializer samples non-colliding start states each episode.
- **Simulation APIs.** Environments expose a Gym-style interface. Sensors return proprioception, RGB, depth, segmentation masks, force-torque readings and the task reward.
- **Controllers.** A composite controller either splits the action vector across body-part controllers (OSC_POSE, OSC_POSITION, JOINT_POSITION, JOINT_VELOCITY, JOINT_TORQUE) or runs whole-body IK on end-effector targets. OSC and joint-position actions can also include stiffness, or stiffness and damping.
- **Devices.** Keyboard, SpaceMouse and MuJoCo GUI teleoperation for collecting and replaying demonstrations.

## Results
- The nine tasks: block lifting, block stacking, pick-and-place, nut assembly, door opening, table wiping, two-arm lifting, two-arm peg-in-hole and two-arm handover.
- SAC baselines on low-dimensional state (500 epochs, 500-step episodes, five seeds, about two days per run on 2 CPUs) solve three of the nine tasks: block lifting, door opening and two-arm peg-in-hole.
- On block lifting and door opening, both Panda and Sawyer learn faster with OSC_POSE than with JOINT_VELOCITY. The authors recommend Panda with OSC as the standard benchmark setting.

## Limitations
The paper has no limitations section. Its benchmark covers one algorithm (SAC) on state observations only. SAC makes slow progress on the other six tasks, which the authors say need better exploration over longer horizons. All results are in simulation.
