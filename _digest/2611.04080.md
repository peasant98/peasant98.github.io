## What they do
Scene maps for mobile manipulation are built before the instruction is known, so they can miss what a task hinges on, such as "the plush toy with a bow tie". FEIJOA is a language-model agent (Gemini-3.8-Flash) that grounds each reference against the raw posed RGB-D scan once the instruction arrives, then acts through a harness that checks each skill is feasible and verifies its effect.

## How it works
- **Task-driven grounding.** A grounding subagent, Fido, turns the instruction into label queries. Its tool runs text-prompted SAM 3 on every scan frame, lifts the masks to 3D with depth and camera poses, merges repeat views into instances and confirms each with Qwen3-VL-8B. The agent inspects the candidates and re-queries until the instruction is resolved.
- **Tools and plan.** The agent also calls navigate, pick, place, push and wipe skills, estimates navigation cost, and keeps a task plan of subgoals it updates from execution feedback.
- **Feasibility-aware sampling.** Base poses facing the target are sampled on an occupancy grid built from the scan, filtered by footprint collision and the skill's reach, and ranked by reach margin and travel cost. If none pass, the tool refuses and says why.
- **Effect verification.** A separate Gemini-3.6-Flash judge sees only the claimed effect and before/after images, and returns success, failure or uncertain.

## Results
- Grounding, 103 queries over four scans: 94.2% exact match, against 68.0% for a captioned scene graph (the best prebuilt map) and 47.6% for the grounding tool alone, at 29.5 s and 3.1 cents per query. A standalone Gemini 3.8 Flash shown every frame reaches 96.1%, but returns only one point per object.
- Spot: 35/50 tasks succeed (13/16 short, 12/18 medium, 10/16 long horizon), 17 of them after recovering from a failure. Of the 15 failures, 13 come from robot skills.
- Ablations: replaying 20 failed place/wipe steps, the agent wrongly marks 9 as done given only status text, 6 with before/after images and 2 with the verifier's verdict. Without cost estimation it drives 9.8% further and takes 12.3% longer.

## Limitations
The robot's physical skills are the main bottleneck. Query-time grounding is slower than looking up a prebuilt map, and language-model calls take 41% of wall time (about 20 per trial at 7 s each). The method needs a prior scan and assumes the scene changes only through the robot's actions. All 50 trials are on Spot.
