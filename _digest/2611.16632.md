## What they do
Frontier models can plan and call tools, but on real robots they struggle with long-horizon, contact-rich tasks, partly because they are blind while the arm moves and while they think. Reins is a harness in which an off-the-shelf model operates two 6-DoF AirBot Play arms through perception tools and atomic actions, with no skill library, task code or robot training. It is tested with GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 on five tasks: a shell game, opening and filling a drawer, folding a towel, folding a shirt, and sweeping with a brush and dustpan.

## How it works
- **Tools.** The model has eighteen tools. `find` (SAM 3 on RGB-D) and `measure` turn an object name or pixel into a 3D point, accurate to about 1.6 mm with the wrist camera. `move_until_contact` stops when joint current indicates contact, and `close_gripper` reports whether anything is held.
- **Guarded chains.** `do` runs up to eight tool calls, each with a check on its result and an optional repeat-until condition, and stops at the first failed check. Model latency is 41-78% of episode time, so each decision covers 1.6-3.4 checked steps on average.
- **Narrator.** A second instance of the model watches the head camera at 1 frame/s, and its account of what changed comes back with every tool result.
- **Twins.** A separate tool fits parametric object models to SAM masks to build a MuJoCo scene from one head-camera frame. It was not used in the episodes.

## Results
- Over five episodes per task, GPT-6.1 Sol succeeds in 88% of real episodes. Without the harness it fails every shell game and takes 8 times as long. The three models perform alike overall, and all three open the drawer every time.
- The shell game, in which the mugs are shuffled while the model waits, separates them. Without the narrator, Sol wins 2 of 5. Sol's narrator answers in 1-6 s and sees a shuffle in 8-11 windows, while Astra's and Opus's take 3-40 s and see it in 2-4. Astra's narrator still tracks the red object in 4 of 5 episodes, Opus's in none.
- In the twins, 11 of 14 movable objects pass the simulation-ready checks with mask IoU 0.90-0.99. A scripted version of each task runs to completion in simulation.

## Limitations
Task knowledge enters only through prompts; the drawer and sweep prompts add grip and pose hints. Contact is sensed only from joint current and gripper width. The evaluation covers one platform, with five episodes per condition and success judged by the operator. A slow narrator reports fast changes late.
