## What they do
Planners need many IK solutions, not one: alternative base placements, starts from which a whole path stays reachable, collision-free postures in clutter. GenGenIK samples whole-body configurations, or trajectories over several targets, conditioned on end-effector targets, a scene point cloud and optional preferences. All experiments are in simulation.

## How it works
- **Masked flow matching.** An encoder-decoder Transformer maps noise to base and joint values. Masks set which targets are active and which coordinates are fixed, so one architecture covers point, multi-end-effector, mobile-base and path IK. A soft bounding function keeps outputs inside joint limits.
- **Data.** cuRobo solutions certified with Drake, from 27 MolmoSpaces kitchens and about 2,500 procedural environments. Scenes enter as a 4096-point labeled cloud encoded into 32 tokens.
- **Speed.** Fine-tuning through the sampler with collision and stability penalties, reflow distillation to one step, then a light Levenberg-Marquardt refiner.
- **Preferences.** Non-differentiable requests (base stand-off, elbow height) are conditioning tokens; differentiable ones (manipulability, stability) use guidance kept in the constraint tangent space.

## Results
- Grasp IK on RB-Y1 in 252 held-out scenes: 80.9% success@1 and 98.2% success@8, versus 29.0% and 58.1% for cuRobo. From a partial cloud: 61.4% and 90.1%, versus 12.0% and 52.1%. With no cloud or another scene's cloud, success@1 falls to 16.7% and 20.7%.
- Path IK (door/drawer, pushing, bimanual lift, flip-up, under-table pickup): 92.7-100% success@32 in 20-36 ms to a first valid solution, versus 22.0-92.0% and 167-1067 ms for cuRobo. Conditioning on the full path makes 58.2% of first configurations extendable to the whole task, versus 24.3%.
- Scene-free point IK: lower position and rotation error than IKDiffuser on FR3, a LEAP hand and G1, with fewer network evaluations. Guidance on G1 raises manipulability 28.42% (IKDiffuser 24.69%) and cuts stability violations 68.74%.

## Limitations
No real-robot experiments. A model trained without under-table pickup scores 0% on it, so it does not extrapolate to new kinds of scene interaction, and each task family still needs expert-solver data. In scene-free point IK, cuRobo has lower latency under large GPU batches.
