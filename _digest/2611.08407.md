## What they do
Social Humanoid makes a 29-DoF Unitree G1 react to a human partner in real time. A camera watches the person, a generative model produces the humanoid's whole-body response and predicts the partner's next motion (also when they are occluded), and NVIDIA's SONIC tracking controller executes it. For training data they retarget human-human motion capture into a 260-hour Human-Humanoid Interaction (HHI) benchmark.

## How it works
- **Adaptive-HHI retargeting.** Pass I retargets one person to the G1 with OmniRetarget's interaction-mesh SQP, keeping wrist and elbow distances to the partner. Pass II adapts the partner's pose to the shorter robot instead of shrinking the partner.
- **Shared-codebook VQ-VAE.** Separate human and humanoid encoders quantize into one 512-entry codebook; two alignment losses make paired poses map to the same code.
- **Diffusion Forcing Transformer.** Autoregressively generates human and humanoid tokens, each noised independently, so the sampling schedule alone switches between joint generation, partner prediction and inpainting.
- **Deployment.** ZED 2i stereo camera, Fast SAM 3D Body for the human pose, AprilTags plus odometry for the robot, SONIC at 50 Hz, most compute offboard.

## Results
- Generated humanoid motion: W-MPJPE 0.128 vs 0.545 for Ready-to-React and about 1.1 for retrieval baselines, which still win on FD and foot skating.
- Retargeting: wrist error 0.50 vs 1.38 cm for OmniRetarget on CHI3D; downstream tracking success on Harmony4D 97% vs 81% (OmniRetarget) and 62% (PHC).
- Real G1, 30 trials per scenario with 4 participants: 100% for handshake, wave, hug, bend and linked arms, 90% for high-five, matching simulation. Median camera-to-command latency is 308 ms.
- The codebook also enables feed-forward shadow teleoperation (2.79 cm human-to-humanoid error).

## Limitations
No hands, faces or objects. The system inherits SONIC's and the pose estimator's failure modes and tracks kinematics without modeling contact forces. Success rates measure execution, not interaction quality; there is no HRI study.
