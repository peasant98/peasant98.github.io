## What they do
Humanoid whole-body teleoperation usually needs motion capture or a VR headset. MoViC needs only a fixed RGB webcam. It reconstructs the operator's body per frame, retargets the motion to the 24-DoF EngineAI PM01 and feeds it to a universal motion tracker trained with PPO on retargeted human motion datasets. The same policy tracks dance and teleoperates object manipulation with no task-specific training.

## How it works
- **Video to reference.** YOLO11-pose finds the person, SAM 3D Body reconstructs the mesh at about 10 Hz on one RTX 5090, a small MLP converts it to SMPL and GMR retargets it to the robot.
- **Frequency-domain context.** A conv encoder with a differentiable FFT turns each 1 s motion window into per-channel frequency, amplitude, offset and phase. Instead of a stacked preview, the actor gets ten frames of proprioception, one next-step target and codes covering about ten future frames.
- **Extrapolation.** A second encoder, trained for multi-step prediction, advances the latent phase to produce 50 Hz references between visual updates. It adds only the predicted increment to the latest measurement and rejects updates inconsistent with its motion model.

## Results
- Ablation: the motion code plus one target reaches 99% success at 0.25 rad MPJPE. Ten explicit future frames reach 95% at 0.28 rad with an 11.1% larger input.
- Extrapolation error is 0.0655 rad joint RMSE vs 0.7561 rad for linear extrapolation.
- Reconstruction on EMDB: 117.54 mm MPJPE vs 123.45 mm for the GVHMR pipeline used by SONIC.
- Tracking an unseen dance from video in MuJoCo, MoViC has the lowest errors on all three metrics against SONIC, OmniH2O and TWIST retrained on the same data. On the real robot it performs the dance, including single-leg poses, a bimanual box transfer and a 139 s bag carry while stepping.

## Limitations
References are only as good as reconstruction and retargeting, and phase extrapolation cannot stay accurate through long occlusions or abrupt motion changes. The camera is fixed, neither module sees objects or contact forces, and the real-robot results are qualitative demonstrations.
