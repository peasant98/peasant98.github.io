abstract: We present Segment Anything Model 2 (SAM 2), a foundation model towards solving promptable visual segmentation in images and videos. We build a data engine, which improves model and data via user interaction, to collect the largest video segmentation dataset to date. Our model is a simple transformer architecture with streaming memory for real-time video processing. SAM 2 trained on our data provides strong performance across a wide range of tasks. In video segmentation, we observe better accuracy, using 3x fewer interactions than prior approaches. In image segmentation, our model is more accurate and 6x faster than the Segment Anything Model (SAM). We believe that our data, model, and insights will serve as a significant milestone for video segmentation and related perception tasks. We are releasing our main model, dataset, as well as code for model training and our demo.

## What they do
SAM 2 is one model for promptable segmentation of images and videos. Clicks, a box or a mask on any frame define an object; the model returns its mask on that frame, propagates it through the video as a "masklet", and accepts corrective clicks on other frames. An image is a one-frame video, where the model behaves like SAM.

## How it works
- **Streaming memory.** A Hiera encoder embeds each frame once. Memory attention conditions the frame on a memory bank holding up to N recent frames, up to M prompted frames and object-pointer vectors from the decoder; a memory encoder writes each new prediction into the bank.
- **Decoder.** SAM's promptable mask decoder, plus a head that predicts whether the object is visible at all (for occlusions) and skip connections from high-resolution encoder features.
- **Data engine.** Annotation time fell from 37.8 s/frame with SAM alone to 4.5 s with SAM 2 in the loop (8.4x). The resulting SA-V dataset has 50.9K videos, 642.6K masklets and 35.5M masks, 53x more masks than any existing video segmentation dataset.

## Results
- Interactive segmentation on 9 zero-shot video datasets: better accuracy than SAM+XMem++ and SAM+Cutie with over 3x fewer interactions.
- First-frame prompts, 17 zero-shot video datasets: 64.7 J&F with one click versus 56.9 and 56.7 for the baselines.
- Semi-supervised VOS (Hiera-B+, 43.8 FPS on an A100): 76.6 on MOSE val versus 71.7 for Cutie-base+, and 76.8 / 77.0 on SA-V val / test where no prior method exceeds 61.4 / 62.8.
- Images, SAM's 23 datasets, trained on SA-1B only: 58.9 mIoU at one click versus 58.1 for SAM, at 130.1 versus 21.7 FPS.

## Limitations
It can fail across shot changes, lose or confuse objects in crowded scenes, after long occlusions or in long videos, and struggles with thin fast-moving details and look-alike neighbours. Objects are processed separately, with no communication between them. The data engine still relies on human annotators to verify masklets.
