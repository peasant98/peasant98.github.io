## What they do
Touch data is expensive to collect and tactile sensors differ in their outputs, so tactile representations are usually trained per sensor. UniTouch trains a single touch encoder on four visuo-tactile datasets from three sensors (GelSight, DIGIT and the Taxim simulator) by aligning touch to ImageBind's frozen image embedding. Since that embedding is already aligned with text and audio, touch gets linked to them without any paired touch-text or touch-audio data.

## How it works
- **Contrastive binding.** A ViT touch encoder is trained with an InfoNCE loss to match each touch image to its paired visual image, encoded by the frozen image encoder.
- **Sensor tokens.** Five learnable tokens per sensor, prepended to the patch tokens, absorb sensor-specific calibration and background color. An unseen sensor uses the tokens of the nearest sensor prototype (mean pixels, L1 distance).
- **Batch sampling.** 75% of each batch comes from one dataset and the rest from others, giving hard negatives within a sensor while still mixing sensors.
- **Applications.** The embedding plugs into off-the-shelf models: text prompts for zero-shot classification, Stable Diffusion unCLIP for touch-to-image, and a LLaMA adapter for a Touch-LLM.

## Results
- Material classification, linear probe: 61.3% on Touch and Go (best baseline 56.5%) and 61.2% on ObjectFolder Real, whose sensor is unseen (ImageNet features 54.8%). Zero-shot with "This feels like [CLS]": 52.7%.
- Grasp stability on Feeling of Success: 82.3% linear probe vs 80.3% best baseline; 65.5% zero-shot.
- Zero-shot retrieval on ObjectFolder 2.0: 41.9, 37.9 and 38.0 mAP for touch to vision, audio and text, vs 32.3, 27.8 and 31.9 for supervised DAR.
- Touch-LLM captions get a GPT-4 rating of 3.30/5 vs 2.33 for LLaVA-1.5. Ablation: naively training on all datasets drops zero-shot Touch and Go accuracy from 43.1% to 21.4%; sensor tokens plus sampling bring it to 52.7%.

## Limitations
Only vision-based tactile sensors are covered; barometric or force sensors are out of scope. Zero-shot accuracy stays below linear probing, and touch-to-image FID (103.1) is worse than supervised Vision-from-touch (81.2). The representation is a black box that is hard to interpret.
