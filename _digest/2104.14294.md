## What they do
The authors ask whether self-supervised pretraining gives Vision Transformers (ViTs) properties that supervised ViTs and convnets lack. With DINO, self-**di**stillation with **no** labels, they find two: the [CLS] token's last-layer attention outlines objects, and frozen features classify nearly as well with k-NN as with a linear layer.

## How it works
- **Student and teacher.** Both networks have the same architecture (a ViT or ResNet backbone plus an MLP projection head) but different weights. Each outputs a K-dimensional softmax, and the student is trained with cross-entropy to match the teacher's output on a different view of the image.
- **Multi-crop.** Two global 224x224 crops and several local 96x96 crops all go through the student. Only the global crops go through the teacher, which encourages local-to-global correspondence.
- **Momentum teacher.** The teacher's weights are an exponential moving average of the student's, and no gradient flows into it.
- **Avoiding collapse.** Only centering (subtracting a running mean of the teacher's outputs) and sharpening (a low teacher temperature). There is no predictor, contrastive loss or batch norm.

## Results
- ImageNet with ViT-S: 77.0% linear and 74.5% k-NN, versus 73.5% and 66.6% for the best of BYOL, MoCo-v2 and SwAV on the same backbone. ViT-B/8 reaches 80.1% linear. On ResNet-50, DINO's 75.3% ties SwAV for the best result among the self-supervised methods compared.
- Attention masks scored against PASCAL VOC12 ground truth: Jaccard 45.9 for DINO ViT-S/16, versus 27.3 for a supervised ViT and 22.0 for a random one.
- With frozen features and no finetuning: DAVIS-2017 video segmentation (J&F)m of 71.4 with ViT-B/8, and 51.5 mAP on revisited Oxford (Medium split) when pretrained on Google Landmarks v2.
- Ablations on ViT-S/16: without the momentum teacher, k-NN accuracy falls to 0.1% (collapse). Replacing cross-entropy with MSE drops it from 72.8% to 52.6%.

## Limitations
Small patches give much of the gain but cost speed: throughput falls from 180 im/s with 8x8 patches to 44 im/s with 5x5. On DAVIS, the 16x16-patch models (61.8, 62.3) score below the self-supervised STC baseline (67.6). Multi-crop raises peak memory per GPU from 9.3 GB to 15.4 GB. Teachers copied from the current or previous-iteration student do not converge. Pretraining used curated ImageNet; uncurated images are left to future work.
