Needle Drop 2

How to play

  1. Press play for a 1-second clip. The needle drops in the middle of the song, not on the chorus everyone knows.
  2. Nobody? Play a longer clip: 3 seconds drops the prize to 2 points, 6 seconds drops it to 1.
  3. The song title wins those points, and naming the artist is worth 1 more. Tap the buttons for whichever team got each.
  4. Reveal to show the cover and play the full 30-second preview. Sing along.
