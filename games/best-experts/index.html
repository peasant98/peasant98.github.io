Best Experts

Everyone at the table is an expert at something. Tonight they prove it, and everyone else gets to steal.

How to play

  1. In an expert round, only the expert answers. Tap the answer they give and the game checks it.
  2. If the expert misses or passes, anyone else can shout the answer and steal it for half the points.
  3. Trivia rounds are open to everyone. Reveal the answer, then tap whoever got it first.
  4. Questions get harder: 100, 200, then 300 points. The final round counts double.
  5. At the end: a Champion for the most points, and a Best Expert for whoever did best in their own category.
