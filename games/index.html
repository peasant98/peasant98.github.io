Before you start

  1. Connect the laptop to the TV and open a game. Press F for full screen.
  2. Name the teams or players on the game's first screen.
  3. For Needle Drop, press "Test the speakers" so the sound comes out of the TV.
  4. Scores save in the browser, so a refresh or a dinner break won't lose your place.
