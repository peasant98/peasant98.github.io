Guess the American movie from the emojis. Three showings, from huge hits to tricky classics.

How to play

  1. Put this on the TV. Teams shout out the movie they think the emojis spell.
  2. Stuck? Open a hint. Each film is worth 3 points, then 2 after one hint, then 1 after two.
  3. Tap the points button for the team that got it first. The title appears, with what each emoji meant.
